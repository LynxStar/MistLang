- **The process boundary** (Requirement 7.5, decision B, Arthur, 2026-09-27). Purity means the same input gives the same output within one process run. The runtime and its version, the globalization mode and ICU, the operating system and the machine are fixed when the process starts, so they are the program's setting, not an input; the current culture, the clock, randomness, environment variables, files and statics that are not Stable_Fields are outside state. Text ordering therefore stays on `StringComparer.InvariantCulture` with nothing pinned in the emitted runtimeconfig, although a pure member sorting text was measured to print `abBiI` normally and `BIabi` under `DOTNET_SYSTEM_GLOBALIZATION_INVARIANT=1`. The review's "ordinal or invariant ordering" and "pin the globalization mode" items are closed by the definition.
